# Staff guide

1. **Find the customer:** type their phone number (at least 4 digits) on the home page. Phone number is their referral code.
2. **New customer:** Register a new customer. Enter name and phone. If someone referred them, enter that person's phone; the page shows whether they are registered. If the referrer is not registered, register them first.
3. **Record a sale:** open the customer > Record service or package. Choose skin analysis (price is shown) or a package (choose it, enter any discount). Enter the amount paid now, method and date, then Record and confirm payment.
4. **Commission:** if the customer was referred and the payment qualifies, a gold message shows who earned what and their new balance.
5. **Instalments:** open the sale (Open next to it) and Record another payment. You cannot take more than the balance.
6. **Wrong entry?** Tell a manager; only managers can reverse payments, void sales or change referral links.
7. Log out when you leave the counter.
